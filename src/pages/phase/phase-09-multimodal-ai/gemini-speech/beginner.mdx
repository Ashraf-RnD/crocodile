## A model that can listen and talk, not just read

Most of the AI systems you've used so far take text in, text out. **Gemini** (Google's
multimodal model family) can take *audio* directly as input, and — through its native
audio capabilities — generate spoken audio directly as output too. No separate
"transcribe, then run the LLM, then synthesize speech" pipeline required.

Think of it like talking to a person instead of typing to a chatbot: it hears your
tone and words together, and can respond in a natural spoken voice, all inside one
model.

### Sending audio to Gemini

```python
from google import genai

client = genai.Client(api_key="YOUR_API_KEY")

with open("question.wav", "rb") as f:
    audio_bytes = f.read()

response = client.models.generate_content(
    model="gemini-2.0-flash",
    contents=[
        "Answer the question asked in this audio clip.",
        {"mime_type": "audio/wav", "data": audio_bytes},
    ],
)
print(response.text)
```

Gemini transcribes and *understands* the audio in one step — no separate speech-to-text
call needed before you can reason about what was said.

## Try it yourself

Ask Gemini to summarize a longer audio clip (e.g. a 5-minute recorded meeting) into
three bullet points instead of transcribing it verbatim.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

```python
response = client.models.generate_content(
    model="gemini-2.0-flash",
    contents=[
        "Summarize this meeting recording in exactly 3 bullet points.",
        {"mime_type": "audio/wav", "data": audio_bytes},
    ],
)
print(response.text)
```

Because Gemini processes the audio natively, it can reason about content, tone, and
structure in the same pass — you're not limited to "transcribe then summarize" as two
separate steps.
