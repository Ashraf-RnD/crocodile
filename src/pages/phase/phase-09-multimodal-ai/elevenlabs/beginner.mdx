## Turning text into a realistic voice

**ElevenLabs** is a text-to-speech (TTS) API known for very natural-sounding, human-like
voices — the kind you often can't immediately tell apart from a real recording. You
send it text, choose a voice, and get back an audio file.

Think of it like hiring a voice actor who can read anything you type, in any of dozens
of preset voices, instantly.

### Your first generated voice line

```python
from elevenlabs import ElevenLabs

client = ElevenLabs(api_key="YOUR_API_KEY")

audio = client.text_to_speech.convert(
    voice_id="JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb",  # a preset voice
    text="Welcome to the multimodal AI lesson.",
    model_id="eleven_multilingual_v2",
)

with open("welcome.mp3", "wb") as f:
    for chunk in audio:
        f.write(chunk)
```

`voice_id` selects which voice speaks the line — ElevenLabs offers a library of preset
voices, and you can browse them (with previews) in their dashboard before picking one
for your project.

## Try it yourself

ElevenLabs lets you control delivery with `voice_settings` — try making the voice
sound more stable and less expressive/variable by adjusting `stability`.

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

```python
audio = client.text_to_speech.convert(
    voice_id="JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb",
    text="Welcome to the multimodal AI lesson.",
    model_id="eleven_multilingual_v2",
    voice_settings={"stability": 0.8, "similarity_boost": 0.75},
)
```

Higher `stability` makes delivery more consistent and monotone; lower values allow
more natural, expressive variation but can occasionally sound less predictable.

</details>
